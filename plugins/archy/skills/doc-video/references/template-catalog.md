# DOC video template catalog

Read this when choosing a template for a DOC video graphic. Every template lives on the `Video` page of `DOC - Videos Foundations`, one row per group. **Split** means the speaker stays in the left half (0 to 960) and the graphic sits on a cream panel in the right half; those templates have an `· Overlay` twin with a transparent left half, which is what goes over the footage. **Full** means the graphic fills the frame.

Each template exists because a moment in the Foundations videos used it. The source column says where (video and timecode), so the original can be checked.

## Not in the catalog on purpose

Full-screen versions of split templates, split versions of full ones, the red transition wipe and the outro lockup were built and then removed: they are not what the videos show. Do not rebuild them unless Design asks for them.
