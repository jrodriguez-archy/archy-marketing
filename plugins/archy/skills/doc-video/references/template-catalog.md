# DOC video template catalog

Read this when choosing a template for a DOC video graphic. Every template lives on the `Video` page of `DOC - Videos Foundations`, which holds templates only. Each section starts with a `§` label artboard, templates run left to right in code order, and every `· Overlay` twin sits directly below its template.

**Split** means the speaker stays in the left half and the graphic sits on the video-ground panel in the right half. **Over footage** means the graphic sits on top of the full-frame speaker shot. **Full** means the graphic fills the frame. Every split and over-footage template has an `· Overlay` twin with the footage removed and a transparent ground, which is what goes into the edit.

The templates come from two sets of Foundations videos: the first nine videos (rebuilt from frames) and the editor's own source files for the F3 videos (rebuilt from the vector files, so their values are exact). When both sets had the same kind of graphic, the F3 version was kept.

## A · Assets

`A · Assets` holds the lockups (Ink, Cream, Symbol Red) and the icon library: 46 icons as `Icon · <name>` frames. Clone from there.

## Not in the catalog on purpose

Full-screen versions of split templates, split versions of full ones, the red transition wipe and the outro lockup were built and then removed: they are not what the videos show. The first-pass title cards (split title card, full red title card, chapter card) were removed too. Do not rebuild them unless Design asks for them.
