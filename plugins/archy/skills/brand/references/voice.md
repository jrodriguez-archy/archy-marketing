# Voice and copy rules

Read this when writing or shortening copy for any Archy piece.

> **Draft.** Written from the copy on existing Archy campaigns. It needs review by Marketing & Design before it is treated as final.

## Who we are talking to

Dental practice owners, office managers and their teams in the United States. Archy is the practice management platform they run the office on. Copy is always in **US English**.

## Tone

- **Direct and warm.** Short sentences, plain words. "Meet Archy at Hinman Dental Meeting", not "We are delighted to invite you to visit our booth".
- **Confident, not loud.** One claim per piece, stated plainly. No stacked superlatives, no exclamation marks in headlines.
- **Human.** Talk about the people in the practice ("Know an office manager who could use a night out?"), not about features in the abstract.
- **Specific.** Real city, venue, date, booth number. Specifics do the persuading.

## Patterns that already work

| Purpose | Pattern | Example from a shipped piece |
|---|---|---|
| Booth invite | `Meet Archy at <event>` / `Join Archy at <event>` | Meet Archy at Hinman Dental Meeting |
| Day-before reminder | `Tomorrow is the day` | Tomorrow is the day |
| Talk or local event | A short two-part idea | Lead with Purpose, Inspire with Culture |
| Share prompt | A question that names the reader's colleague | Know an office manager who could use a night out? Share this |
| Hook | Uppercase, short, `+` to join two offers | WIN PRIZES + BULLS TICKETS |
| AI lockup | `Meet Archy Intelligence™` | Meet Archy Intelligence™ |

## Mechanics

| | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Event names | Official name, as the organiser writes it | `Hinman Dental Meeting 2026`, `Midwinter Meeting 2026` |
| City | `City, ST` with the USPS state code | `Atlanta, GA` |
| Date range | Month spelled out, en dash with spaces between days | `March 12 – 14, 2026` |
| Date and time | Middle dot between date and time, lowercase `am`/`pm` | `February 24, 2026 · 6:00 pm` |
| Booth | `Booth #1039` in running text; on the badge, `BOOTH` over `#1039` | |
| Labels | Title case in the layer, set uppercase by the style | `Location`, `Date & Time`, `Speaker` |
| Trademarks | `™` on `Archy Intelligence` at its first and most prominent use | |
| Brand name | `Archy`, never `ARCHY` in running copy (the wordmark and the deck meta line are the exceptions) | |

**Pick one date format per piece and keep it identical across Post, Stories and OG.** Existing campaigns mix `March 12 – 14` and `March 12–14`; the spaced version is the standard.

## When copy does not fit

Shorten the copy; never shrink the type. Cut in this order: adjectives, then the secondary line (venue before city), then the whole optional block. Propose the shorter version to the requester before applying it.

## Never

- Invent facts: dates, booth numbers, speaker names, prizes. If a fact is missing, ask.
- Change copy the requester supplied, other than to fit a slot, and then only with their approval.
- Write copy in any language other than US English.
