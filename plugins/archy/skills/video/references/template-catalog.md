# Archy video template catalog

Read this when choosing a template. Every template is on the `Templates` page of `Master - Videos`, in Royal, Navy and Ice. **Full** fills the frame; **Over footage** sits on the speaker shot and has an `· Overlay` twin with a transparent ground, which is what goes into the edit.

## O · Openers (first frame of a video)

| Template | Layout | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `O1 · Episode` | Full | Wordmark top left, moon spiral from the top right, index line (number + series) and a big two-line title bottom left |
| `O2 · Centered Hero` | Full | Sky badge with the series name, a very large centred title, small wordmark bottom centre |
| `O3 · Big Number` | Full | A giant episode number in sky beside the series line and title |
| `O4 · Stacked Boxes` | Full | The title split into three stacked boxes, large wordmark bottom right |
| `O5 · Speaker` | Full | Title centred vertically, speaker photo (square) with name and role at the bottom |
| `O6 · Moons` | Full | Series name and a short very large title on the left, moon spiral on the right |

## T · Titles

| Template | Layout | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `T1` to `T8` | Full | Text that holds the screen on its own, one or two lines: centred, two tones, one word, moons (T4), boxed word, editorial, diagonal, big number |
| `T9 · Statement` | Full | A statement on the left with the moon spiral on the right |

## CH · Chapters

| Template | Layout | What it holds |
|---|---|---|

| `CH2 · Chapter Split` (+ Overlay) | Over footage | The chapter card on a panel beside the speaker |

## LT · Lower Thirds

## HL · Highlights (supporting points, not the speech)

| Template | Layout | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `HL1 · Highlight` (+ Overlay) | Over footage | One or two lines in a box, bottom left |
| `HL2 · Highlight Bars` (+ Overlay) | Over footage | Each line in its own bar, stepped like pixels |

## SB · Subtitles (the literal speech)

No themes: each comes in `White` and `Yellow` (`subtitle-yellow`), with an `· Overlay` twin.

| Template | Size | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `SB1 · Subtitle` | 1920 × 1080 | One line (two at most), Onest Medium 44/56, centred, 84px from the bottom |
| `SB2 · Vertical Subtitle` | 1080 × 1920 | Onest Medium 60/76, centred, 400px from the bottom |
| `SB3 · Feed Subtitle` | 1080 × 1350 | Onest Medium 48/62, centred, 96px from the bottom |

## Q · Quotes

| Template | Layout | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `Q1 · Quote` (+ Overlay) | Over footage | A line said on camera with a pixel quote mark and the attribution |

## P · Product

Real UI rebuilt from the Product UI Kit (Open Sans, product tokens), on the theme ground.

| Template | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `P1 · Product Focus` | One UI card centred |
| `P2 · Screen Frame` | A screen recording in the app frame |
| `P3 · Framed Screen` | Screen recording with a title |
| `P4 · Speaker Inset` | Screen recording with the speaker in a corner inset and a name tag |
| `P5 · UI Zoom` | A card zoomed in with a cursor on the action |
| `P6 · Message Typing` | A message composer mid-typing |
| `P7 · Feature Token` | A feature name in a breaking-edge box with a NEW badge |
| `P8 · Stat Card` | A title beside a data card |
| `P9 · Notification Corner` / `P10 · Notification Side` | A notification arriving, in a corner or beside the title |
| `P11 · Comparison Table` | Competition vs. Archy, row by row |
| `P12 · Tip` | A stepped TIP tag and one line beside a screen |
| `P13 · Kinetic Line` | One line revealed word by word |
| `P14 · Tile Row` | A label and a row of numbered tiles, one active |

## D · Diagrams

| Template | What it holds |
