# DOC voice and copy

Read this when writing, fitting or shortening copy for a DOC piece.

## Who we are talking to

Dentists in the United States who want to own a practice, start one or buy one, and would rather not learn everything the expensive way. Copy is always **US English**.

## Tone

- **Plain, not clever.** Say the thing: "Nobody taught you business." No metaphor, no wordplay, no exclamation marks.
- **Specific, not vague.** "36 modules. 189 lessons. About 26 hours." Numbers where a lesser brand would use adjectives, and only real numbers.
- **Always against something.** "Not a Facebook group. Not a buddies WhatsApp thread. No sponsored content." The claim names what DOC is not.
- **Second person, dry.** Wry is allowed; a joke carries the idea, it does not decorate it.

## Patterns that already work

| Purpose | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Core claim | Setup in Regular, payoff in Bold | Dental school taught you teeth. / **We teach you business.** |
| Track claim | Name the track's pain, then what DOC teaches | Don't buy a practice blind. / **We teach you what to look for.** |
| Collective | Owners teaching owners | Nobody should own alone. / **Join the owners who teach.** |
| Body line | Two sense units broken after the comma | From the lease to your first patient, / taught by owners who opened theirs. |

## CTA by theme

| Theme | CTA | Line that sets it up |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations | **Join the waitlist** | Founding member access / Become a founding member. |
| Startup | **Enroll now** | Enrollment is open. |
| Acquisition | **Register now** | Registration is open. |
| Dark | **Apply today** | Applications are open. / Join the next cohort. |
| Light | **Get started** | Three tracks, one collective. / Start wherever you are. |

- "Join the waitlist" and the founding-member copy are **Foundations only**: the waitlist is temporary, so templates that all say it go stale.
- Event pieces keep their own CTA: **Save your seat** (a live session), **Save your spot** (a save the date).
- No full stop on a CTA label.
- The line above a CTA should set up its verb (open, then Enroll; registration, then Register).

## Mechanics

| | Rule |
|---|---|
| Brand name | `DOC` in running copy; `Dental Ownership Collective` in full where it is introduced |
| Separator | Middle dot `·` between facts (`Free · 12 modules`). The Save the Date offer line uses a bullet `•` (`Foundations track • Free`). Keep one separator per piece |
| Dates | Month spelled out in running copy; the compact `M.DD` form (`10.12`) only as a graphic numeral |
| Site | `dentalownership.com` |
| Line breaks | Break at a sense unit, not at a width. Avoid a one-word line |

## When copy does not fit

Make it fit in this order: rewrap, then resize the type a little, then shorten. When the new copy is shorter than the copy a layout was tuned for, **grow the type until the longest line fills the column** rather than leaving the measure half empty. Avoid hyphenated words in lines set with `text-wrap: balance` or `pretty` (both break at hyphens); reword instead.

When the requester asks for another line of copy, choose one, fit it and report it; do not send a menu first.

## Never

- Invent a stat, price, module count, date, quote, testimonial or name. Specimen figures are marked as specimens and listed as pending.
- Change the meaning of copy the requester supplied. Fitting it is fine; rewriting it is their call.
- Mention Archy as anything other than a sponsor.
